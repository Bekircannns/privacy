Privacy Policy for Paper Fold

Effective date: November 12, 2026
App: Paper Fold (Android package com.altunsoftware.mekfold)
Publisher: Loopy Digital
Contact: bekircanaltuntas96@gmail.com

1. Who we are and what this covers

Paper Fold is a casual puzzle game for Android, published by Loopy Digital. This policy explains what the game stores on your device, what leaves your device, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the current test version of the game distributed through Google Play. Google provides the advertising, consent and purchase services described below and has its own privacy policy.

2. The short version

  • The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or location, and does not create an account.
  • Your progress (levels, coins, items, settings) is saved on your device.
  • This version shows no ads. Advertising is switched off in this build. The Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (consent) libraries are still included in the app package, so we describe them below and declare them in our Google Play Data safety form.
  • Purchases, if you make any, are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • The game does not send gameplay or usage data to our own servers in this version.
  • You can delete the data stored on your device with "Delete my data" in Settings, or by uninstalling the game.

3. What data is involved

3.1 Stored on your device only

  • Your saved game: progress, coins, owned items, rewards state, settings and your privacy choices.
  • Anonymous gameplay events (for example level started or finished), a random installation ID that is not derived from your advertising ID or device, and short crash notes may be written to a file on your device. In this version the game does not send this file anywhere.

3.2 Libraries from Google included in the app

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob, "GMA Next-Gen"). Included in the app package, but ads are switched off in this version: the game does not configure the SDK or request ads. Google's documentation states that, when it is used, this SDK automatically collects and shares with Google the device IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), app interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information (such as launch time and hang rate) and device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID), for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, encrypted in transit. Because the SDK is part of the app, our Google Play Data safety form declares these data types. If a later version shows ads, we will update this policy first.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires, a consent form from Google may be shown. Your answer is stored on your device; showing the form contacts Google, which receives your IP address, to get the correct form for your region.
  • Google Play Billing. If you buy something, Google Play processes the payment with your Google account. The game receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play and checks it on your device. It does not receive your payment details and does not send your purchases to our servers.
  • Google Play Install Referrer. This library can read, from the Google Play Store app on your device, how the game was installed (for example which store campaign led to the install). It does not send anything itself, and in this version the game does not send this information to our servers.

We do not use any third-party analytics or crash-reporting services (no Firebase, no Crashlytics).

3.3 Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access (for the Google services above), vibration (haptic feedback), wake lock and foreground service (used by Android system libraries), basic phone state, the Google Play billing permission and the advertising ID permission (declared by the ads library). It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

4. Why we use data

Data stored on your device is used to run the game: to keep your progress, settings and purchases. We do not use your data for advertising in this version, and we do not sell your data.

5. Your choices

  • Delete my data: Settings → Delete my data removes your progress, saved settings, the on-device event file and the random installation ID, and resets your privacy answers. Purchases stay with your Google account and can be restored.
  • Privacy options: where required, Settings → Privacy options reopens Google's consent form.
  • Android controls: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Purchases: manage purchases and refunds in Google Play; use "Restore purchases" in Settings.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the game removes all data it stored on your device.

6. Third parties

The only third-party services built into the game are from Google (Mobile Ads, User Messaging Platform, Play Billing, Play Install Referrer). See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

7. Children

Paper Fold is not directed at children and does not ask for your age. It is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you are a parent and believe a child has used the game, contact us at bekircanaltuntas96@gmail.com; we will explain how to delete the data on the device.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you use "Delete my data" or uninstall the game. The on-device event file is size-limited and the oldest entries are removed first.
  • Data Google processes is kept under Google's own policies.
  • To ask us anything about your data, write to bekircanaltuntas96@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

9. Changes

If a later version shows ads or sends gameplay data to our servers, we will update this policy at this address before that version is released, and ask for your consent where the law requires. Data that leaves your device through the Google services above is encrypted in transit (TLS).

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, US state privacy laws or Turkey's KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the game does not know who you are, most of this happens on your device with the controls in section 5. For anything else, write to bekircanaltuntas96@gmail.com.
